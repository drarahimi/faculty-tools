MEng is a course-based graduate degree (8 courses, no thesis/prereq chain), so this view lists courses by category instead of a term sequence. Sourced primarily from the official Fall 2026 Graduate Academic Calendar (the university's approved, permanent course catalog), cross-checked against the live MAME Fall 2026 Graduate Course Offerings page for the handful of sections actually confirmed this term. A few departments (Mechanical, Materials, Industrial, Environmental) also have a single flexible "Special Topics / Directed Studies" course number whose specific content rotates by section – those appear as one umbrella card listing illustrative past/current topics rather than as separate fixed courses. There is no dedicated Aerospace graduate stream at UWindsor — the MEng program areas are General/Mechanical, Automotive (including the newer MEng in Automotive Engineering's AUTO-8xxx courses), Materials, Environmental, and Industrial & Systems. Solid teal cards are confirmed offered Fall 2026 (with instructor/schedule); dashed cards are real calendar courses whose current-term offering wasn't confirmed — verify with the department before registering.
