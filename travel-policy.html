University Operating / PD Funds vs. Tri-Agency Research Grants

Understand the critical audit differences before charging travel to research grants

Policy Category University Operating & WUFA PD Federal Tri-Agency (NSERC / SSHRC / CIHR)
Boarding Passes Proof of Travel Flexible
Itinerary, detailed e-ticket, or hotel folio proving presence at destination is usually accepted if pass misplaced. 		Strictly Mandatory
Original boarding passes (physical stubs or digital wallet screenshots) are required by federal auditors to prove travel took place.
Research Justification Linkage General academic or professional development purpose stated on the expense report. Explicit Objective Link: Must clearly explain how the conference or fieldwork directly advanced the specific goals of the funded research project.
Airfare Class of Service Standard economy airfare. Seat selection and one checked bag reimbursable. Premium economy requires Dean approval. Lowest Logical Economy Airfare: Business or premium economy is entirely ineligible. Upgrades are personal expenses.
Alcohol & Hospitality Alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable. Business hosting allowed with prior VP approval and detailed attendee list. Strictly Ineligible: Alcohol cannot be charged under any circumstances. Any personal entertainment is completely disallowed.
Combined Personal / Vacation Travel Permitted if business trip is primary. Must print a flight quote for direct business-only dates on the day of booking. Strict Pro-Rating: Grant will only pay the lesser of the business quote vs. actual ticket. Zero incremental personal lodging/meals may be charged.
Rental Car CDW Insurance
Universal University Rule: Inside Ontario, CDW is optional (covered by university/credit card). Outside Ontario or international, purchasing the agency's Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) is mandatory.
